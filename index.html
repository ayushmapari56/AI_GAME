AI Architecture & Mathematical Foundations

How Supervised, Unsupervised & Graph Search algorithms solve the hunter-prey pursuit problem.

PILLAR 1

A* Pathfinding Algorithm

f(n) = g(n) + h(n)

  • g(n): Exact path cost from hunter's current coordinate to cell n.
  • h(n): Admissible Manhattan distance heuristic $h(n) = |x_1 - x_2| + |y_1 - y_2|$.
  • Priority Queue: Evaluates minimum $f(n)$ candidate nodes dynamically.
  • Zone Edge Modulation: Adjusts step cost $w(u, v)$ based on unsupervised cluster properties.
PILLAR 2

K-Means Zone Clustering

J = \sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{x \in S_k} ||x - \mu_k||^2

  • Feature Vectors: [Visit Frequency, Dwell Duration, Enemy Proximity, Open Passages, Coordinates].
  • Cluster 0 (Green): Safe Zone with low player footprint and minimal enemy patrols.
  • Cluster 1 (Yellow): High-transit connecting corridors and junctions.
  • Cluster 2 (Red): Dangerous Hotspots and frequent encounter choke-points.
PILLAR 3

Supervised Predictor

P(Y_{t+1} \mid X_t) \rightarrow \text{Lookahead } (x', y')

  • Online Ensemble: Continuous feature extraction from player movement lag windows.
  • Sensory Inputs: 4-directional wall radar, enemy relative vector, previous $k$ steps.
  • Lookahead Interception: Projects $N$ steps along predicted heading and feeds the interception coordinate $(x', y')$ directly to A*!
